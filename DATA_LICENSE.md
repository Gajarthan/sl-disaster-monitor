# Data rights and attribution

The MIT license applies to this project's original source code only.

DMC listing metadata, document titles and short extracted text retain any rights held by their original publishers. Source PDFs remain on the official DMC servers and are linked rather than redistributed. This independent project does not assert an open-content license over government reports or grant rights beyond those held by the source publishers. Check the original report and contact its publisher for uses requiring permission. Machine-derived factual classifications are supplied without warranty and with their original provenance.

District boundaries are a separate derived database under **Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0 (ODbL)**, based on OpenStreetMap contributors / Wambacher via geoBoundaries. Preserve attribution and comply with ODbL when redistributing or modifying the geometry database. See [data/GEOGRAPHY.md](data/GEOGRAPHY.md) for exact source revision, simplification, and licensing links.
